# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces six key concepts related to formal organization of melodic sentences:

1. **[The Musical Sentence as Succession of Periods](ch11-musical-sentence.md)** - The fundamental principle that sentences comprise successions of distinct periods organized around a central theme

2. **[Song-Sentence (Phrase Musicale)](ch11-song-sentence-structure.md)** - A three-period structure with theme, middle period, and final period creating ABA' form

3. **[Commentary (Commentaire)](ch11-commentary.md)** - Melodic development of theme fragments through rhythmic, melodic, and harmonic variation

4. **[Binary Sentence](ch11-binary-sentence.md)** - A four-period structure alternating theme and commentary in ABAB' pattern

5. **[Ternary Sentence](ch11-ternary-sentence.md)** - A five-period structure creating symmetrical arch form with central commentary flanked by thematic statements

6. **[List of Melodic Periods (Catalog of Historical Models)](ch11-melodic-periods-catalog.md)** - Extensive catalog demonstrating transformation of diverse historical sources through Messiaen's compositional language
