# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces six foundational concepts related to melody and melodic contours:

1. **[Melodic Supremacy and Harmonic Truth](./ch08-melodic-supremacy.md)** - Melody as primary generative element with harmony chosen to fulfill melodic implications
2. **[Intervallic Preferences: Descending Augmented Fourth and Major Sixth](./ch08-intervallic-preferences.md)** - Specific intervals forming foundational elements of melodic language
3. **[Melodic Cadence Formulas](./ch08-cadence-formulas.md)** - Characteristic patterns derived from historical models functioning as closural gestures
4. **[Folk Song Influences](./ch08-folk-song-influences.md)** - Melodic characteristics from Russian and French folk traditions
5. **[Plainchant Contours](./ch08-plainchant-contours.md)** - Melodic patterns derived from Gregorian chant and liturgical traditions
6. **[Hindu Ragas](./ch08-hindu-ragas.md)** - Melodic modes from Indian classical music providing non-Western resources
