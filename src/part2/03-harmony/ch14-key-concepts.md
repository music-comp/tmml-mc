# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces eight key concepts expanding Messiaen's harmonic vocabulary:

1. **[The Chord on the Dominant](ch14-chord-on-dominant.md)** - A saturated harmonic structure containing all seven pitch-classes of the major scale, functioning as an enriched dominant sonority

2. **[The Chord of Resonance](ch14-chord-of-resonance.md)** - A harmonic structure derived from the acoustically perceptible overtones of a low fundamental pitch, forming the basis of "natural harmony"

3. **[The Chord in Fourths](ch14-chord-in-fourths.md)** - A harmonic structure built from stacked perfect and augmented fourths, abandoning tertian construction

4. **[Effects of Resonance](ch14-resonance-effects.md)** - Compositional techniques employing chord clusters to simulate or evoke acoustic resonance phenomena

5. **[Clusters of Chords](ch14-chord-clusters.md)** - Multiple chords sounding simultaneously, creating dense harmonic textures and coloristic stratifications

6. **[A Look at Other Styles](ch14-other-styles.md)** - Analytical examination of how other composers' harmonic languages can be understood through Messiaen's theoretical framework

7. **[Natural Harmony](ch14-natural-harmony.md)** - A conception of harmony as emerging from acoustic phenomena and spiritual aspiration rather than theoretical systems

8. **[A List of Connections of Chords](ch14-chord-connections.md)** - A systematic catalog of harmonic progressions demonstrating practical applications (Examples 230–301)
