# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces five key concepts reframing traditional nonharmonic tone categories:

1. **[Enlargement of Foreign Notes into Groups](ch15-enlargement-concept.md)** - The transformation of traditional single-note dissonances into multi-note structures that function as complete, self-contained musical units

2. **[The Pedal Group](ch15-pedal-group.md)** - A sustained or repeated musical structure, foreign to the surrounding harmonic context, possessing its own internal musical organization

3. **[The Passing Group](ch15-passing-group.md)** - A musical structure that reproduces the terms of a harmonic progression symmetrically while functioning analogically to traditional passing motion

4. **[The Embellishment Group](ch15-embellishment-group.md)** - A complex ornamental gesture that elaborates a single structural pitch while functioning as a complete musical unit

5. **[Upbeats and Terminations](ch15-upbeats-terminations.md)** - The structural articulation points that frame and define musical gestures, forming the upbeat-accent-termination complex
