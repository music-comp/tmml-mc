# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces six key concepts establishing the modes of limited transpositions:

1. **[Theory of the Modes of Limited Transpositions](ch16-theory.md)** - Pitch collections formed from symmetrical intervallic groups that can only be transposed a limited number of times before reproducing the same pitch-class content

2. **[First Mode of Limited Transpositions (Whole-Tone Scale)](ch16-mode1.md)** - A six-note collection transposable only twice, equivalent to the whole-tone scale

3. **[Second Mode of Limited Transpositions (Octatonic Scale)](ch16-mode2.md)** - An eight-note collection consisting of four symmetrical groups, transposable three times, corresponding to the octatonic or diminished scale

4. **[Third Mode of Limited Transpositions](ch16-mode3.md)** - A nine-note collection transposable four times, consisting of three symmetrical groups of four notes each

5. **[Modes 4, 5, 6, and 7](ch16-modes4-7.md)** - Four additional modes transposable six times, each divided into two symmetrical groups, offering distinctive harmonic colors

6. **[Relation of Modes of Limited Transpositions and Nonretrogradable Rhythms](ch16-modes-rhythms-relation.md)** - A fundamental structural parallel between symmetrical properties of modes and nonretrogradable rhythms, creating a "theological rainbow"
