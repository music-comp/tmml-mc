# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces three key concepts demonstrating the flexibility of modal practice:

1. **[Relation to the Major Tonality](ch17-relation-major-tonality.md)** - The technique of combining modes of limited transpositions with traditional major-key tonality through emphasis or mixing of materials

2. **[Modulation of a Mode to Itself](ch17-modulation-to-itself.md)** - The technique of changing transposition levels within a single mode, creating harmonic motion while maintaining the mode's characteristic structure

3. **[Modulation of a Mode to Another Mode](ch17-modulation-to-another.md)** - The technique of moving from one mode to a different mode, creating large-scale harmonic transformation within the modal system
