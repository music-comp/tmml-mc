# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces four foundational concepts related to nonretrogradable rhythms:

1. **[Retrograde Rhythms](./ch05-retrograde-rhythms.md)** - Temporal reversal of rhythmic patterns creating inversions in the temporal domain
2. **[Nonretrogradable Rhythms (Palindromic Rhythms)](./ch05-nonretrogradable-rhythms.md)** - Symmetrical structures that read identically forwards and backwards
3. **[Relation of Nonretrogradable Rhythms and Modes of Limited Transpositions](./ch05-relation-to-modes.md)** - Fundamental analogy between temporal and pitch-class symmetries
4. **[Central Common Values](./ch05-central-common-values.md)** - Pivot points serving as axes of symmetry in palindromic structures
