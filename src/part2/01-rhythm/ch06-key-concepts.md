# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces eight foundational concepts related to polyrhythm and rhythmic pedals:

1. **[Gathering into Meter](./ch06-gathering-into-meter.md)** - Notational practice of organizing ametric music within conventional metric frameworks
2. **[Superposition of Rhythms of Unequal Length](./ch06-superposition-unequal-length.md)** - Layering patterns of different durations that cycle until realignment
3. **[Superposition upon Different Forms of Augmentation](./ch06-superposition-augmentation.md)** - Presenting patterns at multiple time-scales simultaneously
4. **[Superposition upon Its Retrograde](./ch06-superposition-retrograde.md)** - Layering a pattern with its temporal reversal
5. **[Rhythmic Canons](./ch06-rhythmic-canons.md)** - Contrapuntal structures with rhythmic imitation independent of pitch
6. **[Canon by the Addition of the Dot](./ch06-canon-dot-addition.md)** - Canonic structures where voices progressively augment through dotting
7. **[Canon of Nonretrogradable Rhythms](./ch06-canon-nonretrogradable.md)** - Canons where all voices employ palindromic rhythms
8. **[Rhythmic Pedal](./ch06-rhythmic-pedal.md)** - Patterns repeating ostinato-fashion independent of surrounding activity
