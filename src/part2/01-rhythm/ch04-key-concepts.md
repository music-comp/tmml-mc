# Key Concepts

This chapter introduces four foundational concepts related to proportional rhythmic transformations:

1. **[Classical Augmentation and Diminution](./ch04-classical-augmentation.md)** - Proportional scaling of rhythmic patterns by constant multiplicative factors
2. **[Addition and Withdrawal of the Dot](./ch04-dot-addition-withdrawal.md)** - Hybrid transformation combining proportional scaling with non-proportional modification
3. **[Systematic Table of Augmentation and Diminution Forms](./ch04-systematic-table.md)** - Comprehensive taxonomy of transformation procedures
4. **[Inexact Augmentations](./ch04-inexact-augmentations.md)** - Simultaneous layers undergoing different rates of transformation
